# E-Commerce-Sales-Analysis
Business Insight and Strategic Recommendations

# Key Business Insights

1. Sales Performance: Sales reached $241.2 million, with month-on-month growth of 0.9%. This indicates positive growth, although there is room for improvement.
2. Product Category: Electronics generated $176 millions in sales, making it the leading category and a major contributor to overall revenue.
3. Customer Segmentation: Millennials emerged as the leading customer segment, highlight an opportunity to better understand their purchasing behaviour
4. Regional Performance: Haryana recorded the highest state-level sales, followed by Uttar Pradesh
5. Delivery Performance: The average delivery time was 4.5 days, making logistics efficiency an important area for further investigation.

# Business Recommendation

1. Accelerate Sales Growth: Introduce targeted promotion, loyalty programs, and customer retention strategies to improve sales performance.

2. Optimize Product Performance: Analyze electronics subcategory, Profit margins, and inventory levels to maximize profitability.

3. Improve Customer Engagement: Develop personalized marketing campaigns based on customer preferences and purchasing behaviour.

4. Explore Regional Opportunities: Investigate successful sales strategies in leading states and identify growth opportunities in underperforming regions.

5. Enhance Delivery Efficiency: Analyze delivery performance by region to identify potential delays and improve customer satisfaction.

# Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analytics can transform raw e-commerce data into actionable insights. By monitoring sales performance, understanding customer behavior, and identifying
operational improvement opportunities, businesses can make more informed decisions and develop strategies for sustainable growth.
